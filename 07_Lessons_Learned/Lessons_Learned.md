# Lessons Learned

## Overview

The Palm Haven Hotel & Resort — Guest Retention Recovery project provided an opportunity to apply Business Analysis concepts and tools to a simulated hospitality business problem.

## Key Learning Areas

### 1. Business Problem Analysis
Understanding how to identify business challenges, investigate their underlying causes, and define the problems that require attention.

### 2. Stakeholder Analysis
Understanding the importance of identifying stakeholders, considering their interests, and documenting their business needs.

### 3. Requirements Documentation
Gaining practical experience in organising business requirements and documenting them in a Business Requirements Document.

### 4. Business Process Modelling
Understanding how AS-IS and TO-BE process models help communicate existing processes and proposed improvements.

### 5. Data Analysis
Applying Excel and Power BI to explore data, calculate performance indicators, identify patterns, and communicate findings.

### 6. Business Case Development
Understanding how to justify a proposed solution by considering business benefits, costs, risks, and expected outcomes.

### 7. Team Collaboration and Project Management
Learning the importance of task coordination, accountability, communication, and progress tracking when working on a group project.

## Personal Reflection

This project strengthened my understanding of the Business Analyst's role in connecting business problems, stakeholder needs, requirements, data insights, and proposed solutions.

It also highlighted areas for continued improvement, particularly in data interpretation, analytical reasoning, requirements development, and communicating recommendations.

## Conclusion

The capstone project provided practical exposure to the Business Analysis process and helped build a foundation for further professional development and portfolio projects.
