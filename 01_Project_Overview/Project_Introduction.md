# Palm Haven Hotel & Resort — Guest Retention Recovery

## 1. Project Overview

Palm Haven Hotel & Resort — Guest Retention Recovery is a Business Analysis capstone project developed as part of the TechCrush Business Analysis Bootcamp, Cohort 8, Group 10.

The project focuses on investigating guest retention challenges within a fictional hospitality organisation and identifying opportunities to improve customer experience, repeat bookings, and corporate customer relationships.

## 2. Project Background

Palm Haven Hotel & Resort is presented as a hospitality group operating multiple properties and serving different categories of guests, including corporate and leisure customers.

The project scenario highlights business performance challenges relating to declining repeat bookings, reduced corporate customer retention, declining guest satisfaction, and increasing customer acquisition costs.

## 3. Business Problem

The organisation's simulated business scenario identifies the following challenges:

- Declining repeat guest bookings.
- Reduced corporate account retention.
- Lower guest satisfaction.
- Increasing customer acquisition costs.
- Gaps in guest experience and service recovery processes.

These challenges create a need to investigate the underlying issues and develop practical recommendations.

## 4. Project Aim

To analyse the factors contributing to declining guest retention and develop business recommendations that can improve guest experience, customer loyalty, and long-term business performance.

## 5. Project Objectives

1. Examine the factors contributing to declining guest retention.
2. Analyse guest behaviour, booking patterns, and customer satisfaction.
3. Identify gaps in existing business processes.
4. Document business requirements for improvement.
5. Develop a business case for the proposed solution.
6. Recommend improvements to the guest journey and service recovery process.

## 6. Project Scope

### In Scope

- Business problem analysis.
- Stakeholder and requirements analysis.
- Guest retention and customer satisfaction analysis.
- Review of simulated business and operational data.
- AS-IS and TO-BE process modelling.
- Business case development.
- Data analysis using Excel and Power BI.
- Business recommendations.

### Out of Scope

The project does not involve implementing a live hotel management system, integrating with actual booking platforms, or analysing verified operational records from a real hotel business.
