# Palm Haven Hotel & Resort — Guest Retention Recovery

**Business Analysis Capstone Project | TechCrush Bootcamp | Cohort 8 | Group 10**

## Project Overview

Palm Haven Hotel & Resort is a fictional hospitality organisation used for this Business Analysis capstone project.

The project investigates declining guest retention, reduced customer satisfaction, weakening corporate account retention, and increasing customer acquisition costs. It applies Business Analysis techniques to understand the business challenges, document requirements, analyse supporting data, and develop recommendations for improving guest retention.

## Business Problem

The project scenario identifies several business challenges:

- Declining repeat bookings.
- Reduced corporate customer retention.
- Declining guest satisfaction.
- Increasing customer acquisition costs.
- Weaknesses in the guest experience and service recovery process.

## Project Objectives

1. Examine the causes of declining guest retention.
2. Analyse customer and operational data.
3. Identify business process and service delivery gaps.
4. Document business requirements.
5. Develop a business case for improvement.
6. Propose a future-state process and recommendations.

## Project Disclaimer

Palm Haven Hotel & Resort is a fictional organisation developed for the TechCrush Business Analysis Bootcamp Capstone Project.

All organisational details, stakeholder names, operational scenarios, survey responses, financial figures, and dataset records are simulated for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes.

This project does not represent an actual consulting engagement or verified performance data from a real hotel business.
