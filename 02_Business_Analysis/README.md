# Business Analysis Deliverables

## Overview

This folder contains the Business Analysis documentation and process models developed for the Palm Haven Hotel & Resort — Guest Retention Recovery capstone project.

The deliverables demonstrate how Business Analysis techniques were applied to investigate the business problem, document requirements, justify proposed improvements, and model the existing and proposed business processes.

## Deliverables

### 1. Business Requirements Document (BRD)
Documents the identified business needs, requirements, scope, and proposed solution considerations.

### 2. Business Case
Presents the business problem, justification for the proposed improvement, expected benefits, financial considerations, and recommendations.

### 3. AS-IS Process Model
Illustrates the existing guest journey and business processes within the project scenario, highlighting areas requiring improvement.

### 4. TO-BE Process Model
Illustrates the proposed improved guest journey and processes designed to address the identified business challenges.

## Purpose

These documents provide the business analysis foundation for the proposed guest retention recovery initiative.

**Note:** Palm Haven Hotel & Resort is fictional. All business scenarios and supporting information are simulated for educational and portfolio purposes.
